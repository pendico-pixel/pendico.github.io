About BNSS

The Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS) is India's criminal procedure law. It replaces the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (CrPC). It governs arrest, investigation, bail, trial, criminal courts, appeals and other criminal procedures throughout India.
